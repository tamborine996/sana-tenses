# The Wonderful Wizard of Oz reader

A phone-first, single-path chapter reader inside English Reading Practice.

## Product contract

- Present the complete public-domain book, one chapter at a time.
- Keep the current chapter automatically in local browser storage.
- Make **Next chapter** the primary action; do not add a library, topic chooser or settings burden.
- Preserve the unabridged Project Gutenberg source text.
- Show the original W. W. Denslow chapter art.
- Work at 320px, 360px and 412px without horizontal overflow.
- Keep chapter navigation keyboard accessible and move focus to the new chapter heading after navigation.

See `SOURCE.md` for provenance and regeneration instructions.
